# Privacy

VimText stores notes locally on your Mac.

- Default note storage is `~/Library/Application Support/VimText/notes/`.
- If you choose a custom notes location, VimText stores notes in that folder instead.
- VimText does not include analytics, telemetry, or network sync.
- Vim yanks and code-block copy actions write text to the macOS clipboard so you can paste it in other apps.
- Launch backups and recovery copies are stored locally under `~/Library/Application Support/VimText/`.

Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete your local notes or backups.
